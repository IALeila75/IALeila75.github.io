RESEARCH

Research Interests

My research interests focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing and Generative AI, with an emphasis on intelligent and reproducible systems.

RESEARCH PROFILE

Artificial Intelligence for intelligent systems

My work explores the design and development of AI-based systems capable of processing data, retrieving relevant information and assisting users in complex tasks.

I am particularly interested in the combination of machine learning models, language models, semantic representations and domain knowledge to build useful and reliable AI applications.
